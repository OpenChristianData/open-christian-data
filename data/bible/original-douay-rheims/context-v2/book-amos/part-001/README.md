## Book Introduction 1

**Head**

THE PROPHECY OF AMOS.

**Book Introduction Text**

*AMOS a herds-man of Thecua in the tribe of Zabulon*, was endued with the spirit of prophecy \[1\] about *the same time with Osee and Joel*, in the reign of Ozias King of Juda, and of Jeroboam son of Joas King of Israel, in Metaphores, and other obscure speeches agreeable to his pastoral education, but profound in sense, *prophesieth* especially *against the Kingdom of Israel, and diverse* \[2\] \[3\] *Gentiles; partly* also *against Juda*, foreshewing their afflictions for their sins: but at last the vocation of all Nations to Christ, with aboundance of spiritual graces in his Church.

**Book Introduction Note 1**

Amos prophesied the same time with Osee and Joel.

**Book Introduction Note 2**

*S. Iero. Epist. ad Paulin.*

**Book Introduction Note 3**
