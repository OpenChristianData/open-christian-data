Roboam dieth and his son Abias reigneth.

**Verse 9 context** — verse wording is in its Bible row.

Source inline cues, in order: note anchor: (a).

**Inline Note a**

Jeroboam did not wittingly and of purpose set up false gods, to the end he might provoke God to anger: for his intention only was to keep the people from going to Jerusalem, lest by that occasion *they should return to Roboam their lord, King of Juda. ch. 12. v. 27.* But by setting up idols he did provoke God consequently to anger. So here and in other places this phrase: *that he might provoke: that it might be fulfilled,* and the like, signifieth not the final cause, but the sequel of other facts, without direct intention.
