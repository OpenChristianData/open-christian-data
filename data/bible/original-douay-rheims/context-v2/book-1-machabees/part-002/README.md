## Chapter 16

**Chapter Summary**

Simon sendeth two of his sons to fight against Cendebæus, 8. whom they overthrow. 11. Ptolomee Simon's son in law, inviting him to a feast treacherously killeth him, and his sons Judas and Mathathias: 19. sendeth also men to kill John, but he being warned of the treason, killeth them: 23. and succeedeth to his father in the government.
