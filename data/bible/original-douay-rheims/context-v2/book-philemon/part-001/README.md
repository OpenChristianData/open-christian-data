## Chapter 1

**Chapter Summary**

Hearing of Philemon's virtue, who was a Colossian, he writeth a familiar letter from Rome (being prisoner there) about his fugitive servant Onesimus: not doubting but that he might command him, yet rather requesting that he will forgive him, yea and receive him as he would Paul himself, who also hopeth to come unto him.

### Chapter Annotations

#### Annotation 5

**Head**

Toward all the saints.

**Annotation Text**

◦ The Apostle sticketh not to say, Charity and faith in Christ and all his Saints, which our captious Adversaries count in Catholic men's speeches and writings, very absurd, feigning that in all such we make no difference betwixt the love we bear to Christ, and the love we owe to our neighbours: betwixt the trust or belief we have in God, and that which we have in his holy Saints. Malice and contention doth so blind all Heretics.

**Annotation Subnote ◦**

Faith and belief in Saints.
