## Book Introduction 1

**Head**

THE PROPHECY OF ZACHARIAS.

**Book Introduction Text**

*ZACHARIAS* the son of Barachias, and Nephew of Addo, beginning two months after Aggeus, \[1\] exhorteth also to re-edify the Temple: &amp; sheweth by diverse visions that the Church shall flourish, partly in that time of the old Synagogue, but much more after Christ's coming: whose first and chief promulgators of his Gospel shall be of the Jewish nation: but the far greater number shall be of the Gentiles, the Jews for their obstinacy rejected. Yet they also in the end shall return to Christ. \[2\]

Zachary began to prophesy but two months after Aggeus.
