**Annotation Text**

◦ He carefully warneth them to take heed of seditious sowers of Sects and dissension in religion, and this ever to be their mark, if they should teach or move them to any thing which was not agreeable to that which they had learned at their conversion: not bidding them to examine the case by the Scriptures, but by their first form of faith and religion delivered to them before they had or did read any book of the new Testament.

**Annotation Subnote ◦**

Against Sect-masters how to examine our faith.

#### Annotation 18

**Head**

But their own belly.

**Annotation Text**

◦ Howsoever Heretics pretend in words and external shew of their sheep's cote, indeed they seek but after their own profit and pleasure, &amp; by the Apostle's own testimony we be warranted so to judge of them as of men that indeed have no religion nor conscience.

**Annotation Subnote ◦**

Heretics given to voluptuousness.

#### Annotation 19

**Head**

Your obedience.

**Annotation Text**

Against Heretics and their illusions, there is no better way than in simplicity to cleave unto that which hath been taught before: for the which the Roman obedience is much commended. *See Annot.* upon the first *chap. vers. 8.*
