## Book Introduction 1

**Head**

THE PROPHECY OF HABACUC.

**Book Introduction Text**

*HABACUC* born in Bezochar prophesied the same time with Nahum, foreshewing the victories of ◦ the Chaldees subduing many nations, namely the Kingdom of Juda, destroying Jerusalem &amp; the temple, &amp; carrying the people captive into Babylon, and afterwards the ruin of the same Chaldees, and relaxation of the Jews: at last the coming of Christ, with diverse particular Mysteries described in a Canticle from his incarnation to the general Judgement, and eternity of the next world.

obscure.

**Book Introduction Note ◦**

Habacuc prophesied of the two tribes: of the Chaldees: and of Christ.
