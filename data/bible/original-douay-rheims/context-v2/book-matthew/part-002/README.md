### Chapter Annotations

#### Annotation 1

**Head**

Judge not.

**Annotation Text**

It is no Christian part to judge ill of men's acts which be in themselves good and may proceed of good meaning, or of man's inward meanings and intentions which we cannot see: of which fault they must beware that are too suspicious and given to deem always the worst of other men. But to say, that Judas, or an Heretic evidently known to die obstinately in heresy, is damned, and in all other plain and manifest cases, to judge, is not forbidden.

Twelve years.

And withal, what a treasure, and pearl it is. 53. After all which, yet his own country will not honour him.
