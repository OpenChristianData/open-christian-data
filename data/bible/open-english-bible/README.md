## Electronic witness scope

Official Open English Bible 2025.6 US and Commonwealth-spelling branches: 44 biblical books in each variant, comprising 17 selected Old Testament books and the full 27-book New Testament. This is a partial Bible.

The text is represented as structured dataset records. Source wording, retained numbering and known omissions remain distinguished from generated routing. Completeness of a historical printed edition is not implied.
