**Edition and provider.** This notice identifies eBible.org's `engwebpb` World English Bible British Edition electronic text. The accepted master binds the ordered 68 extracted USFM textual members: 66 books, front matter (FRT), and glossary (GLO). The retained ZIP is the acquisition package; its three non-USFM members are support material. See the [eBible.org edition page](https://ebible.org/find/show.php?id=engwebpb).

**Attribution and provider rights statement.** Credit eBible.org as the provider of this retained electronic edition. The retained acquisition receipt reports eBible's statement that the edition is public domain and not copyrighted, and identifies the World English Bible name as a trademark. This records the provider's claim. OCD has not independently cleared every underlying right or jurisdiction and does not claim trademark or public publication clearance.

**Projection scope.** The private offline dataset projects 31,103 source verse carriers and their attached notes and source-bound chapter continuations. FRT, GLO, headings, markers, and four nonverse notes remain in the accepted TEI master; they are not additional verse rows. Five source-numbered note-only empty verse carriers remain empty. Source-native labels are preserved without inventing normalized reference targets. The retained electronic text has not been collated against a print edition. Human proofreading, rights decisions, and public release remain separate.

For the visible notice accompanying the data, see `data/bible/engwebpb/README.md`.
