# World Messianic Bible — source and reuse

This selected text is the retained [eBible World Messianic Bible](https://ebible.org/engwmb) USFM electronic witness. The source copyright page labels it an August 2022 stable text using U.S. spelling; the retained support HTML also names its own later generation date. The accepted scope is the complete ordered 66-book USFM text, with 31,103 verse carriers and no FRT or GLO member. The ZIP package and its three nontext support members are retained source support, not additional public verse text.

The public text selection consists of verse rows. The accepted private master also retains book-level headings, source markers, and three notes outside verse rows. Source-native identifiers are not claims of normalized cross-edition Bible references.
