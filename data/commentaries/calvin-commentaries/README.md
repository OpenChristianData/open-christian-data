# Calvin's Collected Commentaries: source credit and rights scope

**Sourced via [CCEL.org](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/calvin/commentaries.html).** Author: John Calvin. This dataset row scope covers historical text and apparatus from the accepted 46-member direct CCEL XML collection, with 45 body components in 19 source-attested title groups. It does not assert a separate authored parent book, print-edition equivalence, or a Bible-verse association.

CCEL's [collection page](https://www.ccel.org/c/calvin/comment2/home.html) names the Calvin Translation Society edition and states that its electronic files are public domain; the retained 46 headers likewise declare `Public Domain`. The [U.S. Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf) describes the U.S. term for older published works. The proposed row license is **Public domain (United States); identified historical text and apparatus only**. This is a bounded U.S. historical-text assessment, not a CC0 dedication or worldwide clearance.

The rows retain commentary, historical source title matter, translator prefaces and notes, and source-ordered textual divisions. Source-native citation labels, division numbers, historical emphasis, Greek/Hebrew labels, and textual roles remain in their structure. The electronic catalogue and component headers stay private. CCEL title-page imprints and URLs (90 paragraphs), image references (10), generated paragraph locator IDs, and CCEL/Word style classes are omitted from public row content. No CCEL image pixels, site descriptions, cover art, files, or formatting derivative are offered. CCEL's [current policy](https://www.ccel.org/about/copyright.html) reserves website and special content and cautions about separately protected additions. The [retained provider permission](../../../docs/sources/ccel-permission.md) permits parsing text but says not to sell CCEL files or derivatives of their formatting. The source is credited here without implying CCEL endorsement.
