# Daily Light on the Daily Path: source and use notice

This dataset edition follows the retained CrossWire SWORD `Daily` module, version 1.0 dated January 1, 2002. Its extracted `daily.dat` and `daily.idx` supply the text and source order. The module configuration supplies metadata and rights claims. The [CrossWire module package](https://www.crosswire.org/ftpmirror/pub/sword/packages/rawzip/Daily.zip) is acquisition packaging, not the text authority. The source contains 366 ordered day entries, including February 29, with a morning and evening reading for each day. The source day labels have no year; this dataset does not assign one.

The retained module configuration titles it *Jonathan Bagster's Daily Light on the Daily Path* and credits Jonathan Bagster (1813–1872) and other members of his family. It calls the work *A Devotional Textbook for Every Day of the Year, In the Very Words of Scripture*. Separately, the retained text header says it was first published in New York by the American Tract Society around 1875. These are claims in the retained electronic source, not an independent identification or collation of an exact print edition. The retained source record attributes the SWORD module to CrossWire and records Bagster Family Bible Publishers as source context.

The module configuration states `DistributionLicense=Public Domain` and says `Public Domain -- Copy Freely`. Those are source-supplied statements. The historical compilation, its electronic arrangement, module redistribution terms, and rights in jurisdictions where that claim may not apply still require separate review before public distribution. Private offline preparation and mechanical acceptance do not establish broader rights clearance, human textual acceptance, or permission for public publication.

The public rows contain only the source-defined morning and evening readings. The 732 source Morning/Evening markers are italicized in the private TEI; the public row text retains their words but flattens that typography. Source line breaks and the literal scripture-reference text and `cRef` values remain in the rows without inferred normalized targets. The complete electronic header remains in the private accepted source representation and is excluded from public rows because it contains historical personal contact details.
