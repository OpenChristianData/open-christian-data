# The Catholic Encyclopedia — qualified historical article text

This private offline selection contains 11,629 cited article pages from the
retained New Advent transcription of *The Catholic Encyclopedia*. New Advent
and each article's named author and transcriber are credited. The page-local
factual citation and approval statements remain with each article. Source
wording and headings are preserved, together with the source bibliography;
site link targets and styling are outside this text selection.

The accepted electronic source also contains 48 navigation or index pages,
three numeric pages without a unique historical article citation, and one
unavailable-source register. Those 52 units are omitted from the selected
records and retained privately. Modern donation and contact messages, site
footer, image pixels and alternative descriptions, and website formatting are
also omitted while remaining in the retained private source. The selected source cohort is
partial: eight declared page requests and nine images were unavailable, and
the missing material has not been supplied from another witness. The article
counts do not describe a complete website or an independently collated print
edition; the historical `vol01` catalog name does not limit the source cohort
to Volume I.

The individual retained citations identify historical United States
publication from 1907 through 1922. This selection treats that historical
article text as public domain in the United States. It does not assert a
license for New Advent's separately authored site material, images, or
formatting, and it does not claim worldwide rights. New Advent's visible
electronic-source credit and article-local contributor credits remain attached.
This package is qualified private offline preparation; public deployment
requires separate authorization.
