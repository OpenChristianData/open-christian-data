# Smith's Bible Dictionary — historical definitions and electronic source credit

This United States-qualified private offline selection contains all 4,560
ordered entries and 5,362 definition blocks in the accepted
[JWBickel/BibleDictionaries](https://huggingface.co/datasets/JWBickel/BibleDictionaries)
electronic witness. JWBickel is credited for supplying the structured JSONL.
Headwords, definition boundaries, literal numbered link markers, and source
order remain as retained; no cross-reference targets or alternate terms are
invented.

The complete selected text was compared with [CCEL's historical electronic
Smith's Bible Dictionary](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/smith_w/bibledict.html).
Its title page identifies William Smith and 1884, and its electronic header
labels the text Public Domain. All 4,560 retained headwords match literally
and all definition word sequences match in order after removing only generated
numeric link labels for comparison. The electronic witnesses differ in
punctuation, spacing, and some glyphs; the retained words were not corrected
or replaced. CCEL has one additional final entry, “Zuzim, The,” absent from
the JWBickel member. This is the complete accepted electronic witness, not an
independently collated complete print edition.

The selected historical definition words are treated as public domain in the
United States. The JWBickel provider card states no reuse license, and this
notice does not invent one or transfer CCEL's file/website rights to that
provider. The full comparison found no new authored definitions in the selected
words. The source JSONL's mechanical structure and numbered link markers are
retained as witness features, without asserting a separate text license for
them. CCEL's presentation and website material are outside the selected rows.
