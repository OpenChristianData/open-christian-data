# Hitchcock's Bible Names Dictionary — historical text and source credit

This U.S.-qualified private offline selection preserves all 2,622 ordered
headword and definition records of the accepted JWBickel/BibleDictionaries
electronic witness. JWBickel is credited for supplying the structured source.
Repeated Abel, Eli, Noah, and Rahab headwords remain distinct records.

Every retained headword and definition matches, in order, the identified
[CCEL historical electronic text](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/hitchcock/bible_names/bible_names.html).
Nine comparisons require only punctuation or spacing differences; their word
sequences are identical, and the retained wording is not silently edited.
The historical text contains an additional “Zuzims” entry absent from the
retained source. The selected dataset is therefore the complete accepted
electronic witness, not a complete independently collated print edition.

[CCEL's title page](https://www.ccel.org/h/hitchcock/bible_names/title.html)
identifies Roswell D. Hitchcock's names dictionary within the New York 1874
edition of *Hitchcock's New and Complete Analysis of the Holy Bible* (copyright
1869). It credits Brad Haugaard for that separate electronic text and expressly
labels it public domain. The matched historical definitions are treated as
public domain in the United States. The JWBickel source has no stated reuse
license; this notice does not invent one or transfer CCEL's electronic-text
status to JWBickel's file as a whole. The structured JSONL is credited as
provenance, while the selected definition words are supported by the complete
historical comparison.

No exact print-exemplar collation, worldwide rights, or public-upload claim is
made. The repository software license does not replace this text disposition.
