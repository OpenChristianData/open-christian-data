# Metropolitan Tabernacle Pulpit: selected historical sermons

This private offline text selection contains 3,548 source-defined rows from the unchanged accepted 3,550-row electronic cohort: 3,547 Kingdom Collective sermon pages and the historical Spurgeon Archive witness for sermon 1698. Accepted supplemental sermons 708 and 3032 are omitted from this public-text output because their Modernized Edition, credited to Larry and Marion Pierce and used by Answers in Genesis by permission, prohibits electronic reproduction and derivatives without express written permission. The selected public dataset rows omit these two modernized witnesses. The thirteen numbered slots 8, 40, 42, 62, 67, 82, 142, 155, 269, 270, 298, 332, and 390 remain unresolved. This is not verified complete print coverage.

The historical Spurgeon sermon text is assessed public domain in the United States. The retained [Kingdom Collective](https://thekingdomcollective.com/spurgeon/) source says its collection was transcribed from manuscripts edited by Emmett O'Donnell and designed by Benry Yip. Credit O'Donnell as manuscript editor, with the retained [SpurgeonGems](http://www.spurgeongems.org/) link, and Yip as designer; the source does not name its transcriber or digitizer. The supplemental sermon 1698 comes from the [Spurgeon Archive](https://archive.spurgeon.org/). Open Christian Data structurally reformatted these retained electronic witnesses without a print collation. Separately copyrightable transcription, editorial contribution, digitization, and formatting rights are not asserted as public domain by the historical-text assessment. This package is a private US-qualified offline preparation, not public deployment or worldwide clearance.

Sermon 1698 retains its literal links and 27 remote image references. The image bytes were not acquired, and visual meaning or missing initial-letter text is not inferred. Readable text and nested structure preserve the accepted source order.
