# Hymnus sanctorum Desiderii et Reginfridi: source credit

Source: Monumenta Germaniae Historica (MGH) and Bayerische Staatsbibliothek (BSB), [openMGH](https://mgh.de/en/digital-mgh/openmgh), Retained edition bibliography: Passiones vitaeque sanctorum aevi Merovingici (IV) Krusch, Bruno Hahn Hannover 1913 Scriptores rerum Merovingicarum 6.

The retained electronic source header licenses MGH and BSB digital annotations under CC BY 4.0, states that the providers claim no rights on the text they believe public domain, and requests credit to both institutions. These are provider statements; independent rights clearance and print-edition equivalence remain unresolved.

The complete source-native Desiderii and Reginfridi hymn in the second part of bsb00000753 work division 2, from title line bsb00000753_00071_10 through Amen line bsb00000753_00071_47. All 43 direct hymn nodes remain in source order: 38 line markers, the title highlight, two column spans, and their two closing anchors. The first prose part remains outside this work. This is the retained electronic scope, without a stronger print-work claim.

Human/print review, final rights disposition, and public publication remain separate.
