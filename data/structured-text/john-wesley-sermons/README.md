# Sermons on Several Occasions: selected text, rights, and source credit

Sourced via [CCEL.org](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/wesley/sermons.html).

The selected ten source-defined units contain five sermon series, 141 sermon divisions, title matter, preface, and factual indexes. The title states that four volumes were published in 1771; many sermon labels identify an 1872 edition. These identify historical material, not a uniform collated print exemplar. The first two series were edited by John Wesley; the last three were assembled without his knowledge, and some sermon authorship is uncertain. One sermon is expressly attributed to Charles Wesley.
