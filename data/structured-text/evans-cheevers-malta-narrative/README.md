# Source and credit: Katharine Evans and Sarah Cheevers' Malta narrative

This electronic text derives from the Text Creation Partnership's A63318
keyboarded and encoded edition of a book printed in London in 1663. It
contains the narrative of Katharine Evans and Sarah Cheevers, together with
the book's title page and preface. The appended account by George Robinson
is a separate work and is not included in this text.

The TCP electronic header states that, to the extent possible under law, TCP
waived copyright and related rights in its keyboarded and encoded edition
under Creative Commons CC0 1.0. The waiver expressly excludes page images
and supplementary files. This text release contains neither of those.
