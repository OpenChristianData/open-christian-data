# A Treatise Concerning Religious Affections: source and rights notice

Jonathan Edwards wrote *A Treatise Concerning Religious Affections*, first published in 1746. The Huntington holds a [1746 title page](https://www.huntington.org/watch-read-listen/verso/religious-affections-colonial-north-america). The retained introduction begins with Edwards's historical opening, while the separate CCEL staff summary is electronic header material and is not a text row. The accepted electronic witness has not been collated to a particular printed exemplar. No translator or modern print editor is identified.

Sourced via CCEL.org (Christian Classics Ethereal Library). The public selection contains seven ordered native rows: Edwards's title, introduction, and treatise in six source divisions, plus a factual scripture-reference index generated in the CCEL electronic witness. The modern cover-art acknowledgment is retained only in the private source and inverse receipt; no cover image is included. Literal wording, Greek, emphasis, small caps, notes, references, and source order remain. Font-family, font-size, and text-alignment declarations from the electronic source have been omitted from public structure.

Edwards's identified historical text is public domain in the United States. The factual index is identified separately as a CCEL-sourced electronic aid, not as a historical printed component. [CCEL's policy](https://www.ccel.org/about/copyright.html) distinguishes underlying public-domain text from its files, formatting, and special contents. The project's recorded CCEL permission allows parsing ThML for text and separately restricts sale of CCEL files or derivatives of their formatting. This notice does not claim worldwide rights or authorize public upload.
