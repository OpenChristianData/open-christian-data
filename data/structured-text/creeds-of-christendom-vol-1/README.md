# Creeds of Christendom I: retained text, credits, and publication disposition

The existing accepted electronic witness contains five source-defined records:
title and dedication, prefatory matter and contents, the historical text, and
two indexes. Its current TEI projection preserves all 2,148,263 text characters
and passes independent source verification and the public privacy screen.
This is a complete retained electronic witness, not a new print collation.

Sourced via [CCEL.org](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds1.xml).
Credit Philip Schaff for the original history and David S. Schaff for the later
revision. The prefatory matter credits Samuel M. Jackson's assistance with the
fourth edition. The retained electronic header credits Charles Bowen for markup;
its transcriber field is blank. The modern description by Emmalon Davis is
outside the accepted five-record text and is not included in the projection.

The source header declares Public Domain and identifies the sixth edition.
The title retains copyright statements for 1877, 1905, and 1919, but the sixth-
edition preface is expressly dated January 1931 and identifies new literature,
denominational notices, and changes to the historical text. Those later changes
are part of the accepted witness. The 1919 title statement alone does not prove
that this entire projected revision was published before 1931. No absence of a
US copyright renewal, worldwide clearance, or additional publisher grant is
asserted from the searches conducted.

The retained CCEL permission permits parsing its XML for underlying public-domain
text and reserves its files and formatting. CCEL's published policy permits
personal, educational, or nonprofit use and directs publishers to obtain
permission for republication or commercial use. These qualifications remain
attached to the retained source; they are not a CC0 dedication or unrestricted
text license. The accepted source, master, and usable private text remain
installed. Full US public selection of the 1931 revision remains held for an
evidence-backed rights decision. This is a publication hold, not missing text,
failed TEI, or an OCR correction task. No public upload has occurred.
