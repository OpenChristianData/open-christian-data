# A Plain Account of Christian Perfection — source and reuse

This private offline text selection represents John Wesley's *A Plain Account of Christian Perfection* from a retained [Christian Classics Ethereal Library (CCEL) ThML witness](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/wesley/perfection.xml). The accepted edition is an electronic reconstruction of that witness; it has not been collated against a printed edition. The source's title page states a period from 1725 to 1777 and refers to *The Works of John Wesley* (Thomas Jackson's 1872 edition, volume 11) as an electronic-source note, not as an independently checked print witness.

The retained electronic header identifies John Wesley as author and Christian Classics Ethereal Library, Grand Rapids, Michigan, as electronic publisher. Its descriptive text is signed "Abby Zwart, CCEL Staff Writer." That is a credit for the source description, not an attribution of Wesley's work to her.

The retained header leaves its `DC.Rights` field empty. A local source configuration labels the underlying work public domain and records permission from CCEL to parse the ThML. These retained statements do not establish rights to redistribute this electronic transcription in every jurisdiction. This package is private and offline; human acceptance, print-edition fidelity, broader rights clearance, and public publication remain unresolved.
