# Roma beata nimis: source credit

Source: Monumenta Germaniae Historica (MGH) and Bayerische Staatsbibliothek (BSB), [openMGH](https://mgh.de/en/digital-mgh/openmgh), *Libelli de lite imperatorum et pontificum*, volume 3, edited by Ernst Dümmler (Hannover: Hahn, 1897).

The retained electronic source header licenses MGH and BSB digital annotations under CC BY 4.0, states that the providers claim no rights on the text they believe public domain, and requests credit to both institutions. These are provider statements; independent rights clearance and print-edition equivalence remain unresolved.

The complete source-native Marbod poem in bsb00000830 work division 23, from its own leading page break bsb00000830_00704 and II. heading bsb00000830_00704_1 through terminal line bsb00000830_00705_10. All 52 source lines and both page breaks ['bsb00000830_00704', 'bsb00000830_00705'] remain in the poem. The shared collection heading, I. marker, and two prior letters remain outside this work. This is the retained electronic scope, without a stronger print-work claim.

Human/print review, final rights disposition, and public publication remain separate.
