# Source and rights notice for the Bishops of Sens letter to Joscerannus

Electronic file source: Monumenta Germaniae Historica (MGH) and the Bavarian State Library, Munich (BSB), digital MGH bsb00000829, *Libelli de lite imperatorum et pontificum*, volume 2.

The electronic source states that annotations added by MGH and BSB are distributed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It says MGH and BSB do not claim rights to the text itself, which they believe is in the public domain.
## OpenMGH license and changes

openMGH provides its TEI-XML resources and the annotations added by the Monumenta Germaniae Historica (MGH) and the Bavarian State Library (Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, BSB) under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). openMGH states that the underlying medieval text is free from copyright. Source: [openMGH](https://www.mgh.de/en/digital-mgh/openmgh).
