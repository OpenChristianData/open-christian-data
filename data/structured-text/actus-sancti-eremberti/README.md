# Source and rights notice for Actus sancti Eremberti pontificis

Electronic text source: openMGH TEI-XML volume bsb00000752, provided by the Monumenta Germaniae Historica (MGH) and the Bavarian State Library, Munich (BSB). See the [openMGH TEI-XML volume list](https://www.mgh.de/en/digital-mgh/openmgh/mgh-editions-in-openmgh). The digital MGH project was funded by the German Research Foundation (DFG).

Printed source: Passiones vitaeque sanctorum aevi Merovingici (III), edited by Bruno Krusch, Scriptores rerum Merovingicarum, volume 5 (Hahn, Hannover - Leipzig, 1910). This derivative selects the complete electronic work division titled Vita Eremberti ep. Tolosani, preserving its prose, styling, source annotations, and page and line markers. The enclosing volume and its other works are outside this edition.

The retained electronic source declares [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) for annotations added by MGH and BSB. Separately, it states that MGH and BSB claim no rights on the underlying text, which they believe is in the public domain. This derivative preserves those distinct statements; it does not relabel the annotations as CC0 or public domain.

Open Christian Data transformed the selected electronic work into accepted TEI and projected it into structured prose rows. No independent print collation or visual source review is claimed. MGH and BSB are credited as the electronic file source as requested by the retained header. This notice documents source statements and the transformation; it does not record a separate rights-clearance or publication decision.
