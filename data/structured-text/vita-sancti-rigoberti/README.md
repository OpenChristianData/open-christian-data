# Source and rights notice for Vita sancti Rigoberti

Electronic text source: openMGH TEI-XML volume bsb00000754, provided by the Monumenta Germaniae Historica (MGH) and the Bavarian State Library, Munich (BSB). See the [openMGH TEI-XML volume list](https://www.mgh.de/en/digital-mgh/openmgh/mgh-editions-in-openmgh). The digital MGH project was funded by the German Research Foundation (DFG).

Printed volume identified in the retained TEI header: *Passiones vitaeque sanctorum aevi Merovingici (V)*, edited by Bruno Krusch, *Scriptores rerum Merovingicarum* 7 (Hannover: Hahn, 1920). The original contents list the Vita on pp. 58–78, the *Translatio a. 894* on pp. 78–79, and the *Translatio brachii* on p. 80 ([original contents scan](https://d-nb.info/790379252/04)). This derivative contains only the Vita section.

The retained electronic source declares [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) for annotations added by MGH and BSB. Separately, it states that MGH and BSB claim no rights on the underlying text, which they believe to be in the public domain, and asks distributors of copies or derivatives to name both institutions. These are the source's distinct statements; this notice does not turn them into a separate rights clearance.

Open Christian Data represents the complete source-native Vita section as TEI and projects it into one structured Latin prose row. The TEI preserves source line and page markers and other structure; plain-text projection is not a substitute for that structure. No independent print collation or visual source review is claimed. This notice is attribution evidence, not a human-acceptance decision or publication authorization.
