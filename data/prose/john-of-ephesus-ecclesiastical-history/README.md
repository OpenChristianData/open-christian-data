# Source credit and license

*Ecclesiastical History* is attributed to John of Ephesus. This item contains only the surviving Syriac Part III. Parts I–II and the English Part III are not represented. The source describes its digital critical edition as preliminary and says it will be updated while the project's English translation and commentary are developed.

The source credits Nikolai Kiel as editor and Simon Birol, Philip Michael Forness, Silvio Roggo, and Hartmut Leppin as contributors. Their listed institutions include Goethe University Frankfurt and KU Leuven. The German Research Foundation (DFG) funded the project. The Berlin-Brandenburgische Akademie der Wissenschaften is the source authority; the Patristic Text Archive (PTA) distributes the 2023 edition.

Source: [PTA reader](https://pta.bbaw.de/en/reader/pta0200.pta001.pta-syc1); [pinned PTA TEI source](https://github.com/PatristicTextArchive/pta_data/blob/ef861ab391ea35c5d85b41dd8d7ba2bf65530b21/data/pta0200/pta001/pta0200.pta001.pta-syc1.xml). CTS URN: `urn:cts:pta:pta0200.pta001.pta-syc1`.
