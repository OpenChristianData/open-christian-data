Source: Monumenta Germaniae Historica (MGH) and Bavarian State Library, Munich (BSB), [openMGH](https://mgh.de/en/digital-mgh/openmgh), electronic witness bsb00000829. The retained bibliography identifies *Libelli de lite imperatorum et pontificum*, volume 2, edited by Ernst Dümmler, Hannover: Hahn, 1892 (MGH Ldl 2).

This derivative selects the complete native part 11, “LIBELLUS XI. DE PRESBYTERIS,” in the source's first work division, including its heading, address, page and line markers, inline formatting, and word correspondences. Neighboring parts and the remaining volume are outside this work's selection. The retained ZIP is an acquisition package; the extracted XML is text authority.
