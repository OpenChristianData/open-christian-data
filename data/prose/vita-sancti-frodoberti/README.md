# Vita sancti Frodoberti abbatis: source credit

Source: Monumenta Germaniae Historica (MGH) and Bayerische Staatsbibliothek (BSB), [openMGH](https://mgh.de/en/digital-mgh/openmgh), Retained edition bibliography: Passiones vitaeque sanctorum aevi Merovingici (III) Krusch, Bruno Hahn Hannover - Leipzig 1910 Scriptores rerum Merovingicarum 5.

The retained electronic source header licenses MGH and BSB digital annotations under CC BY 4.0, states that the providers claim no rights on the text they believe public domain, and requests credit to both institutions. These are provider statements; independent rights clearance and print-edition equivalence remain unresolved.

The entire separately titled Vita Frodoberti abb. Cellensis direct child work division, ordinal 4 of 26, in extracted openMGH bsb00000752 XML is accepted only as the retained electronic witness. The source verse prologue and prose Vita remain together in their whole-work division without recategorizing or rewriting either part. Other sibling works and the volume shell remain in the retained source. No print collation, rights clearance, or public release is claimed.

Human/print review, final rights disposition, and public publication remain separate.
