# York Tractate: De consecratione pontificum et regum

Source and digital annotations: Monumenta Germaniae Historica (MGH) and the Bavarian State Library, Munich (BSB), through the digital MGH project funded by the German Research Foundation (DFG).

The electronic witness is the openMGH TEI-XML volume bsb00000830, representing Libelli de lite imperatorum et pontificum, volume 3, edited by Ernst Dümmler, published by Hahn, Hannover, 1897. See the [openMGH TEI-XML volume list](https://www.mgh.de/en/digital-mgh/openmgh/mgh-editions-in-openmgh). The electronic publication statement is dated November 28, 2016.

The source declares its added digital annotations available under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and claims no rights on the text itself, which it believes to be public domain. This derivative preserves the native electronic text and structure in a work-specific TEI and projects that accepted master to structured data. Credit MGH and BSB when distributing copies or derivatives.

The whole surviving electronic fourth tractate is represented. Its last lines announce a comparison of priestly and royal consecration, then end literally with “consecratio regis......”. No missing conclusion or gap markup has been invented. Completeness and the cause of this breakoff in the original composition, print edition, or manuscripts remain unresolved. This is a technically checked reconstruction of the retained electronic witness; human review, print collation, rights clearance, and publication authorization are separate.
