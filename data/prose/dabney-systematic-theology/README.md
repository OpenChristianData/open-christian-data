# Source and selection: Dabney's Systematic Theology

Electronic source: [Monergism Books EPUB](https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/dabney/Systematic%20Theology%20-%20R.%20L.%20Dabney.epub).

The selected historical text includes the note to the reader, second-edition preface, nine sections, 48 chapters, and appendices A and B. Duplicate ebook navigation and provider packaging are excluded. The full EPUB remains in the private source record. The U.S. public-domain finding applies to the historical Dabney wording, not later creative additions or EPUB packaging. Exact print-edition collation has not been performed.
