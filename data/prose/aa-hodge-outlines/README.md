# Source and selection: Outlines of Theology

Electronic source: [Monergism Books EPUB](https://www.monergism.com/thethreshold/sdg/aahodge/Outlines%20of%20Theology%20-%20A.%20A.%20Hodge.epub).

The selected text is Hodge's two authorial prefaces, all 43 chapters, and the historical appendix. Provider navigation, cover, and 2018 copyright/license pages are excluded from the public text; the complete EPUB and its notice remain in the private source record. The U.S. public-domain finding covers historical Hodge wording, not later creative material or EPUB packaging. Exact print-edition collation has not been performed.
