Source: [Patristic Text Archive](https://pta.bbaw.de/) (PTA), from the retained three-tome electronic edition of Eusebius Caesariensis. The source headers name PTA as distributor and Berlin-Brandenburgische Akademie der Wissenschaften as authority.

- Tome I, Psalms 1–50: editors Barbara Villani, Sergey Kim, Benjamin Gleede, and Magali Coullet; [PTA text](https://pta.bbaw.de/en/reader/42f98b11/pta0003.pta020.pta-grcBibex1).
- Tome II, Psalms 51–100: editors Cordula Bandt and Magali Coullet; [PTA text](https://pta.bbaw.de/en/reader/pta0003.pta020.pta-grcBibex2).
