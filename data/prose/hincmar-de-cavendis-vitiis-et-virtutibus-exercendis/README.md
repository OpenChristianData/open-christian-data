# Hincmar, De cavendis vitiis et virtutibus exercendis — source credit

Source: Monumenta Germaniae Historica (MGH) and Bavarian State Library, Munich (BSB), [openMGH](https://mgh.de/en/digital-mgh/openmgh), electronic witness bsb00000629. The retained bibliography identifies *Hinkmar von Reims, De cavendis vitiis et virtutibus exercendis*, edited by Doris Nachtmann, Munich: Monumenta Germaniae Historica, 1998, *Quellen zur Geistesgeschichte des Mittelalters*, volume 16.

This derivative preserves Hincmar's dedication to Charles the Bald (source part A), the complete moral tract with all nested chapters (C), and Hincmar's closing address (D) in source order in one TEI. It retains the source headings, page and line markers, mixed inline hierarchy, and word correspondences. Source part B independently identifies Gregory I's letter to Reccared and is represented under its separate canonical work. The ZIP is an acquisition package; extracted XML is text authority. OCD reconstructed these complete Hincmar parts as TEI and projected the ordered native content into JSONL and Arrow.

The retained source header licenses annotations added by MGH and BSB under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), states that these institutions claim no rights on the text itself believed public domain, and asks copies or derivatives to credit MGH and BSB. This notice fulfills that credit request. It preserves distinct source layers without asserting independent clearance of the 1998 edition or suitability for a selected public release profile.

This is a private offline technical rehearsal. Visual review, human acceptance, print collation, rights clearance, global release selection, and publication authorization remain separate.
