# Source credit and license

*De fide et lege naturae* is a Greek digital critical edition attributed in the source to Severianus Gabalensis (Severian of Gabala). The source names Annette von Stockhausen as editor, the Berlin-Brandenburgische Akademie der Wissenschaften as authority, and the Patristic Text Archive (PTA) as distributor. The digital edition is dated 2018.

Source: [PTA reader](https://pta.bbaw.de/en/reader/pta0001.pta001.pta-grcBibex); [pinned PTA TEI source](https://github.com/PatristicTextArchive/pta_data/blob/ef861ab391ea35c5d85b41dd8d7ba2bf65530b21/data/pta0001/pta001/pta0001.pta001.pta-grcBibex.xml).
