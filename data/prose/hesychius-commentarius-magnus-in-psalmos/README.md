# Hesychius, Commentarius magnus in Psalmos: source credit and license

Greek electronic witness of *Commentarius magnus in Psalmos* by Hesychius of
Jerusalem (Hesychius Hierosolymitanus). Publication authority:
Berlin-Brandenburgische Akademie der Wissenschaften. Distributor:
