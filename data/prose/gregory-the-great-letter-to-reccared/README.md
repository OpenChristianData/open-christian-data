# Letter to Reccared — source credit

Source: Monumenta Germaniae Historica (MGH) and Bavarian State Library, Munich (BSB), [openMGH](https://mgh.de/en/digital-mgh/openmgh), electronic witness bsb00000629. The retained bibliography identifies *Hinkmar von Reims, De cavendis vitiis et virtutibus exercendis*, edited by Doris Nachtmann, Munich: Monumenta Germaniae Historica, 1998, *Quellen zur Geistesgeschichte des Mittelalters*, volume 16.

This derivative selects Gregory I's complete Letter to Reccared in native part B, retaining the German title, Latin address, page and line markers, inline formatting, and word correspondences. Hinkmar's neighboring dedication, moral treatise, and closing word remain outside this letter.

The retained source header licenses annotations added by MGH and BSB under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), states that these institutions claim no rights on the text itself believed public domain, and asks copies or derivatives to credit MGH and BSB. This notice fulfills the source credit request. It preserves the distinct source layers without asserting independent clearance of the 1998 edition or suitability for a selected public release profile.

This is a private offline technical rehearsal. Visual review, human review, print collation, rights clearance, global release selection, and publication authorization remain separate.
