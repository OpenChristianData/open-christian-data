# Adalbert and Bernald, Second Letter to Bernard: source and data notice

This work's data derives from the openMGH TEI-XML volume bsb00000829 of *Libelli de lite imperatorum et pontificum*, volume 2 (MGH Ldl 2), edited by Ernst Dümmler and published by Hahn, Hannover, in 1892. The electronic source was provided by the Monumenta Germaniae Historica (MGH) and the Bavarian State Library, Munich (BSB); see the [openMGH TEI-XML volume list](https://www.mgh.de/en/digital-mgh/openmgh/mgh-editions-in-openmgh).

The selected text is one complete native section in source division 1, part 2: the letter headed `[EPISTOLA III. Adalberti et Bernaldi ad Bernhardum.]`, bounded by source line identifiers `bsb00000829_00055_25` and `bsb00000829_00066_33`. Its canonical work identity calls it the Second Letter to Bernard; the source's group numbering is preserved. Other letters, the preceding shared group heading, the separately labeled fragment, and the volume shell remain in the retained source. This data represents the selected electronic witness, without independent print collation or modernization.

The source electronic publication statement applies the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license to annotations added by MGH and BSB in the DFG-funded digital MGH project. It states that the providers claim no rights in the underlying text, which they believe to be in the public domain. These are distinct source layers: the annotation license is not a new public-domain declaration for every layer or a determination for every jurisdiction. MGH and BSB request that copies and derivative versions identify both institutions as the source. This notice supplies that credit. The license reference is https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ and the project reference is https://www.dmgh.de/.
