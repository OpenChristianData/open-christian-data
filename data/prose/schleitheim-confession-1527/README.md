Electronic source credited to Anabaptists.org electronic transcription of the Rod and Staff printing. U.S. selected historical wording: public domain. The Rod and Staff sixth-printing date identifies the retained printing, not the origin of the translation. The accepted master and Anabaptists.org source remain complete.
