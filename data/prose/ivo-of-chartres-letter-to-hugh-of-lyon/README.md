# Ivo of Chartres, Letter to Hugh of Lyon — source credit

Source: Monumenta Germaniae Historica (MGH) and Bavarian State Library, Munich (BSB), [openMGH](https://mgh.de/en/digital-mgh/openmgh), electronic witness bsb00000829. The retained bibliography identifies *Libelli de lite imperatorum et pontificum*, volume 2, edited by Ernst Dümmler, Hannover: Hahn, 1892 (MGH Ldl 2).

This derivative preserves Ivo's complete letter addressed to Hugh, Archbishop of Lyon, through its closing Valete. The collective Epistolae heading is recorded as source context, and the subsequent separately addressed Bishops of Sens letter and Joscerannus response are outside this letter. It retains the source page and line markers, wording, inline formatting and word correspondences. The ZIP is an acquisition package; extracted XML is text authority. OCD reconstructed the complete letter as TEI and projected its ordered native content into JSONL and Arrow.

The retained source header licenses annotations added by MGH and BSB under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), states that these institutions claim no rights on the text itself believed public domain, and asks copies or derivatives to credit both institutions. This notice fulfills that credit request and preserves the distinct source layers without asserting independent rights clearance or suitability for a selected public release profile.
