# Source credit and license

*Vita sancti Cononis Bidanae* is a Greek critical edition whose source identifies the author as “Anonymos.” It was edited by Philipp Pilhofer, with the Berlin-Brandenburgische Akademie der Wissenschaften as authority and the Patristic Text Archive (PTA) as distributor. The digital edition is dated 2024.

Source: [PTA reader](https://pta.bbaw.de/en/reader/pta0041.pta001.pta-grc1); [pinned PTA TEI source](https://github.com/PatristicTextArchive/pta_data/blob/ef861ab391ea35c5d85b41dd8d7ba2bf65530b21/data/pta0041/pta001/pta0041.pta001.pta-grc1.xml). CTS URN: `urn:cts:pta:pta0041.pta001.pta-grc1`.
