# Olaudah Equiano, *Interesting Narrative*: electronic source credit

This private dataset selection contains 23 ordered prose units from the
complete first and second volumes of Olaudah Equiano's *The Interesting
Narrative of the Life of Olaudah Equiano: or Gustavus Vassa, the African.
Written by Himself*, second edition, London, 1789. It represents the retained
Text Creation Partnership (TCP) ECCO electronic transcription. Volume 1 has
14 units and volume 2 has nine. Notes and page markers remain in each unit's
nested structure but are omitted from its readable plain text.

Credit the [Text Creation Partnership ECCO project](https://github.com/Text-Creation-Partnership/ECCO-TCP)
for the keyboarded and encoded text and the University of Michigan Library for
its [volume 1](https://name.umdl.umich.edu/004837188.0001.001) and
[volume 2](https://name.umdl.umich.edu/004837188.0001.002) item records.
The TCP repository describes its encoded texts as CC0 1.0 Universal. The
retained 2008 volume headers also contain older distribution-permission
wording. Their relationship has not been resolved by a human rights review;
this notice does not assert independent rights clearance or public
publication authorization. Commercial Gale page images are not included.

The electronic text preserves explicit transcription gaps and has not been
collated against the historical print images. Human textual acceptance and
exhaustive proofreading remain unrecorded. Preserve this TCP and Michigan
credit with any generated data from this edition.
