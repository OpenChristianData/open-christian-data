# Evangelische Gezangen (1806): selected Dutch hymn texts

Source: *Evangelische Gezangen, om nevens het Boek der Psalmen bij den openbaren godsdienst in de Nederlandsche Hervormde Gemeenten gebruikt te worden*, first edition, Johannes Allart, Amsterdam, 1806, through the DBNL electronic text. Credit: “geraadpleegd via DBNL (KB, nationale bibliotheek).” DBNL marks this title *Auteursrechtvrij* and says it may be downloaded and redistributed digitally or in print for any purpose: https://www.dbnl.org/titels/gebruiksvoorwaarden.php?id=_eva005evan01.

This release selects 192 source-defined body hymns in source order, one row per hymn; hymn XLVI keeps its six labeled sections. DBNL describes the electronic text as a diplomatic representation of the 1806 first edition with three documented interventions. Its page 151 replacement for illegible text is the conventional `[...]` lacuna marker in hymn LXXIII, retained so no missing historical words are invented. The page 352 additions to a register and page 386 spelling correction are outside these hymn rows. See DBNL's colophon: https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_eva005evan01_01/colofon.php.

The selection omits title matter and two prefatory divisions; back hymn lists, the tune and alphabetical registers, 26 index or register tables, and final errata; 68 score GIF images and their associated back-matter poem groups; and source display formatting and page-break markers. These remain in the accepted private source/master where present, but are not packaged as selected text. The electronic text has not been collated against print page scans. The rights description is limited to this historical text selection and its factual lacuna notation, not to the omitted images or DBNL's wider collection.
