# Book of Common Prayer 1662: selected historical text

This private offline United States selection contains 25 source-defined members of a partial electronic Book of Common Prayer witness: 20 services and five calendar/collect parts. It is not the complete printed 1662 Book of Common Prayer.

The retained electronic source and accepted master include three passages that source comments identify as wording added in the 1992 edition: one in Morning Prayer, one in Evening Prayer, and one in Holy Communion. The selected public rows omit those exact passages with a reversible private receipt. The other 22 row texts are unchanged. Source centering is also omitted; all 255 semantic speaker-role annotations, rubrics, textual alternatives, and continuation relations remain. No image payloads are included or licensed by this selection. Five stored missing-page responses remain source evidence outside the selected text.

Source credit: [retained electronic BCP1662 witness](https://www.eskimo.com/~lhowell/bcp1662/). The source does not establish the provider or transcriber identity or contain an explicit electronic-text permission notice. No particular printed copy has been collated against this partial electronic witness. The selected license is “Public domain (United States) for identified historical prayer-book text in the partial 1662 electronic witness; the three expressly identified 1992 additions are excluded.” This qualification does not establish worldwide rights: English Crown rights require a separate jurisdictional assessment. This packet does not authorize public upload or commercial redistribution. The separately accepted BCP1662 collects dataset is unchanged.
