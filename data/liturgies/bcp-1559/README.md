# Book of Common Prayer 1559: selected historical text

This private offline United States selection contains 15 public rows from a complete 16-member retained electronic expression. It is a partial source-defined witness, not a line-by-line collation of a printed 1559 book. The selected rows retain historical prayer-book services, 1561/1604 additions, the separately dated James I proclamation, and Godly Prayers drawn by this source from 1567, 1599, and 1634 witnesses. Godly Prayers is contextual material often bound with prayer books; it was never officially part of the 1559 BCP. Short factual variant and source notes remain with credit.

The Web author's modern Elizabethan overview, comparative State Prayers heading and table, two explanatory paragraphs about Godly Prayers, two cross-reference notes to the omitted comparison, repeated site navigation and footer, and decorative image references are omitted from the selected rows. A private exact inverse retains the complete accepted source, all 16 rows, and the original HTML/link annotations. The public rows preserve historical wording and hierarchy, relevant named anchors, table spans, source-image labels, and two image-source links as source context. Image pixels, PDFs, and linked-edition text are not included.

Source credit: [Justus Anglican BCP 1559 electronic witness](http://justus.anglican.org/resources/bcp/1559/). Fifteen source-page footers identify Charles Wohlers as Web author. The provider's Notes on the Text say the electronic pages were transcribed from the 1890 Griffith Farran Okeden and Welsh edition and identify a 1634 source for 1604 changes; those statements have not been independently collated against print. The inherited Richard J. Mammana transcription credit remains unconfirmed and is not asserted here. The selected license is “Public domain (United States) for identified pre-1931 prayer-book text, related historical prayers and proclamation; short factual source notes retained with credit, while modern Web-authored overview and comparative state-prayer presentation are excluded.” This does not clear every layer of the electronic edition. English Crown rights outside the United States and public upload require separate review.
