# Hymnus de gloriosissimo Christi martyre Gangulfo

Source and digital annotations: Monumenta Germaniae Historica (MGH) and the Bavarian State Library, Munich (BSB), through digital MGH, funded by the German Research Foundation (DFG).

The electronic witness is the openMGH TEI-XML volume bsb00000754, representing Passiones vitaeque sanctorum aevi Merovingici (V), Scriptores rerum Merovingicarum volume 7, edited by Bruno Krusch, published by Hahn, Hannover, 1920. See the [openMGH TEI-XML volume list](https://www.mgh.de/en/digital-mgh/openmgh/mgh-editions-in-openmgh). The electronic publication statement is dated November 28, 2016.

The source declares its added annotations available under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and claims no rights on the text itself, which it believes to be public domain. This derivative preserves the electronic hymn's native wording, mixed text, line markers, and two column spans in a work-specific faithful TEI, then projects the accepted master to native poetry data. Credit MGH and BSB when distributing copies or derivatives.

The hymn comprises five numbered stanzas ending with Amen. The separately titled following Vita is excluded. The literal source heading is “[HYMNUS DE GLORIOSISSIMO CHRISTI MARTIRE GENGULFO.”; its closing bracket occurs in the following Vita heading. No closing bracket or corrected spelling has been added. The source reading “Cleinenterque” remains unchanged. The catalog title above serves as the existing work identity label; it is not a claim that the source heading was spelled identically.

This is a technically checked reconstruction of the entire retained electronic hymn. Print collation, close visual comparison, human review, independent rights clearance, and publication authorization remain separate and unrecorded.
