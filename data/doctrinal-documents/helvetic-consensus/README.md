# Source and rights: Helvetic Consensus, Hodge 1879 appendix

The selected text is the complete English Formula Consensus Helvetica in Appendix II of A. A. Hodge, *Outlines of Theology* (New York: Robt. Carter & Bros., 1879), printed pages 656–663. [Internet Archive copy outlinesoftheolo1879hodg](https://archive.org/details/outlinesoftheolo1879hodg), from Princeton Theological Seminary Library. All eight pages were independently compared to the transcription; the 26 canons and closing prayer are present. The historical 1879 text and apparatus are public domain in the United States. This notice does not identify a translator who is not named by the source. Scan pixels are not distributed in the selected text row. Rights outside the United States require separate assessment. The distinct Klauber electronic edition is excluded from this selection.
