# Westminster Shorter Catechism: Westminster Standard text witness

This selected edition contains 107 ordered questions and answers of the historical Westminster Shorter Catechism and 401 short Scripture-reference occurrences as rendered in the retained Westminster Standard HTML. Credit The Westminster Standard as the electronic source: https://thewestminsterstandard.org/westminster-shorter-catechism/. The public item license applies to the identified historical Q&A wording and factual reference locators in the United States, not to the provider's modern website.

The Westminster Assembly Project catalogs early text-only and proof-bearing printings: https://westminsterassembly.org/principal-documents-of-the-assembly-facsimile-editions/principal-documents-the-shorter-catechism/. The 401 retained reference occurrences have not been checked one by one against the early proof-bearing printing. They are citation locators only; no Bible verse quotations or commentary are included. The selected Q&A keeps source spelling and wording, including Q54's “reverent” and Q32's absence of “and” relative to the separately retained Creeds.json edition. It has not been silently changed to match that witness or an unverified print edition.
