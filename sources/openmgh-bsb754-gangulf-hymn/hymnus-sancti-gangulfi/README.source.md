# Gangulf hymn retained source and distribution credit

The whole five-stanza hymn is reconstructed from the retained openMGH bsb00000754 electronic witness. Its source-literal title begins with an opening bracket. The source puts the closing bracket in the following Vita title; the reconstruction records that context and does not invent a closure, correct the source spelling, or import neighboring prose. Both native column spans and their anchors remain intact.

The electronic publication statement and print bibliography are preserved in the TEI. MGH and BSB request source credit when distributing copies or derivatives. The selected human-visible data notice is `data/poetry/hymnus-sancti-gangulfi/README.md`.

The source assigns CC BY 4.0 to annotations added by Monumenta Germaniae Historica and the Bavarian State Library, Munich, under digital MGH, funded by the German Research Foundation. It claims no rights on the text itself, believed to be public domain. These are retained source statements; independent rights clearance, human/print review, and publication authorization remain separate.
