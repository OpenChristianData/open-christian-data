# A Selection from his Letters: retained source and rights

This accepted electronic witness is sourced via [CCEL.org](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/rutherford/letters.html). It contains Samuel Rutherford's letters in a selected, abridged collection, along with a title page, contents, foreword, glossary, and scripture index. The accepted source has 71 letter divisions; it is neither all surviving Rutherford correspondence nor a verified complete historical-letter edition.

The foreword is signed Hugh Martin. It says Martin chose representative letters, omitted passages, arranged the selection, wrote information about some correspondents, and supplied the glossary. Editorial prefaces also occur inside the letter divisions. The foreword names an earlier complete edition by "Dr Andrew Boner" (source spelling); the accepted member does not identify its text or apparatus as that edition. Emmalon Davis wrote the CCEL electronic description in the source header, outside the six projected body rows.

The retained header gives a CCEL electronic creation date of 2000-07-09, but leaves print publication, source edition, and `DC.Rights` blank. A [Folger catalog record](https://catalog.folger.edu/record/12455) identifies a Hugh Martin edited selection published by SCM Press in 1957; no print collation establishes that the retained bytes are exactly that exemplar. The legacy structured-text config's 1891 Andrew Bonar edition claim is not adopted as accepted-source lineage.

[CCEL's policy](https://ccel.org/about/copyright.html) permits personal, educational, or nonprofit use of its books and asks for contact before republication or commercial use. The retained project [permission record](../../../docs/sources/ccel-permission.md) permits parsing CCEL XML for underlying public-domain text while reserving CCEL files and formatting. Neither statement clears Martin's selection, abridgment, foreword, in-letter commentary, or glossary for this public dataset. No new permission has been obtained.

The whole accepted master and six-row private technical release remain usable. Public selection is held until an exact historical-letter scope can be source-bound without Martin's creative layer, or a sufficient permission for the current selection is established. This is a publication boundary, not a missing-TEI or OCR defect. The downloader-visible notice is maintained at `data/prose/rutherford-letters/README.md`; the work-local disposition is `library/works/rutherford-letters/publication/rights-scope.json`.
