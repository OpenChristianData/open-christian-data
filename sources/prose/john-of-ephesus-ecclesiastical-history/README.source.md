# John of Ephesus source and release notice

This accepted edition represents only the surviving Syriac Part III of *Ecclesiastical History*, from the exact PTA TEI member at pinned repository commit `ef861ab391ea35c5d85b41dd8d7ba2bf65530b21`. The source identifies editor Nikolai Kiel and contributors Simon Birol, Philip Michael Forness, Silvio Roggo, and Hartmut Leppin; it names Goethe University Frankfurt and KU Leuven among their affiliations, the Berlin-Brandenburgische Akademie der Wissenschaften as authority, PTA as distributor, and the German Research Foundation (DFG) as funder. The source describes this digital critical edition as preliminary.

The source's file-level license is CC BY-SA 4.0. OCD's accepted TEI adds a source/work binding and a revision entry while preserving the source header, prologue, apparatus, and body. The offline release projects the accepted TEI into the prose row schema. The downloader-visible notice, including the limited text scope and preliminary-edition statement, is [`data/prose/john-of-ephesus-ecclesiastical-history/README.md`](../../../data/prose/john-of-ephesus-ecclesiastical-history/README.md); it must accompany any selected item.
