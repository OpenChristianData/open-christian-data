The unchanged accepted electronic master contains 3,550 source-defined sermon rows: 3,547 Kingdom Collective HTML pages and supplemental retained witnesses for sermons 708, 1698, and 3032. The two omitted witnesses remain in private accepted rows and in a reversible private omission receipt for independent verification.
