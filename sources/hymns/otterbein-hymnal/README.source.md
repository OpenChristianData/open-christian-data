# Otterbein Hymnal retained electronic witness

The source is the already retained Project Gutenberg 16455 HTML witness of *The Otterbein Hymnal: For Use in Public and Social Worship*, prepared by Edmund S. Lorenz, United Brethren Publishing House, Dayton, Ohio, 1892. The retained archive and cover are separately accounted support. No print exemplar or page-image collation is claimed.

The complete accepted TEI preserves the electronic header, title matter, introduction, contents, 548 hymns, first-line index, and Gutenberg license footer. Public hymn rows select only the 548 source hymn divisions. Source arguments and bylines within a hymn remain in its notes; a single attached byline is also supplied as contributor. The Cowper byline outside hymn 186 is not reassigned. Terminal punctuation at hymns 469 and 470 is retained.

Source credit and public limits must accompany projected rows in `data/hymns/otterbein-hymnal/README.md`; the offline selection binds that notice into its dataset card. The source's Gutenberg notice is evidence of source terms, not a broader grant or a territorial rights determination. Human verification, rights review, final selection, and publication authorization remain separate.
