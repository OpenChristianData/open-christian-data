# Calvin's Collected Commentaries: retained source and publication scope

The accepted electronic collection under the existing `calvin-commentaries` work contains the retained CCEL `commentaries.xml` catalogue and `calcom01.xml` through `calcom45.xml`. Its 45 text components remain in catalogue order within 19 source-attested title groups. The shared catalogue and component electronic headers remain in the private master and receipt. The collection is an electronic witness container, not a separately authored parent book or a claim of print equivalence. The separately accepted Joshua edition remains a distinct, overlapping projection of `calcom07.xml`.

CCEL's [collection page](https://www.ccel.org/c/calvin/comment2/home.html) identifies the Calvin Translation Society edition and expressly calls its electronic files public domain. Every retained member reports `Public Domain` in its source header. The retained catalogue/first two component headers also mention Baker, 1996; that is a print-source/electronic witness statement, not evidence that the historical translations were first published in 1996. The body title pages preserve their historical translator and editor credits. [U.S. Copyright Office Circular 15A](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf) states that works published in the United States before January 1, 1931 are in the U.S. public domain. This is a U.S. historical-text assessment; it does not decide Australian or worldwide rights.

The proposed public extraction includes historical commentary, source title matter, translator prefaces and notes, and source-ordered divisions. It omits the shared electronic catalogue, 45 electronic headers, 90 CCEL title-page imprint/URL paragraphs, and 10 CCEL image references. It strips generated paragraph locators and CCEL/Word style classes while preserving visible historical text, semantic paragraph/table/note/reference structure, source-native citation labels and division numbers, historical emphasis, Greek/Hebrew source labels, and source-native links. No image pixels, site descriptions, cover art, CCEL files, or formatting derivative are offered as public text. The original accepted master retains all these excluded source layers.

CCEL's [current policy](https://www.ccel.org/about/copyright.html) reserves its website and special content, warns that some introductions and cover art may be separately protected, and distinguishes U.S. from other countries' rights. The [retained April 1, 2026 permission](../../../docs/sources/ccel-permission.md) permits parsing ThML/XML for text, separates underlying public-domain text from CCEL file/formatting rights, and says not to sell CCEL files or derivatives of their formatting. Credit the source as **sourced via CCEL.org**. Neither source-reported public domain nor this extraction is a CC0 dedication or public deployment authorization.
