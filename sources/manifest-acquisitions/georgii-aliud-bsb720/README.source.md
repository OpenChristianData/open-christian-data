# Second Epitaph for Georgius, from Epitaphium senioris Georgii triplex

Source: Monumenta Germaniae Historica (MGH) and Bavarian State Library, Munich (BSB), retained openMGH MGH SS rer. Germ.73, bsb00000720. Complete second six-line epitaph with literal ALIUD heading, Carmina ab children96:104. Native internal page break remains intact; no lines or column markers invented. Shared triplex heading is context; other epitaphs are excluded. This is one independent complete poem, not the whole triplex.

The electronic header licenses MGH/BSB annotations under CC BY4.0, does not claim rights in the underlying text, and requests MGH and BSB credit on copies or derivatives. Carry human-visible source/license attribution to `data/structured-text/lambertus-epitaphium-georgii-aliud/README.md`. Human review, print equivalence, rights clearance and publication remain separate.
