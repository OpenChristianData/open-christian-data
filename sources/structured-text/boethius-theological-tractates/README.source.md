# Boethius theological tractates reconstruction source

This nonaccepted TEI candidate is reconstructed deterministically from the retained `raw/ccel/boethius/tracts.xml` witness. It represents exactly `ThML.body/div1[@id='iv']` and all five of that division's direct `div2` tractates, in source order. Sibling `div1` works (`i`, `ii`, `iii`, and `v`) are explicitly excluded and are not copied into the candidate.

The authoritative local config identifies *The Theological Tractates* by Boethius and credits H.F. Stewart and E.K. Rand as the 1918 translators. No additional author, translator, edition, textual, or rights claim is inferred.

The retained electronic witness is sourced via CCEL.org: <https://www.ccel.org/ccel/boethius/tracts.xml>. CCEL permits parsing its ThML; do not sell CCEL files or derivatives of CCEL formatting. The local source config labels the underlying text public domain. These statements remain evidence, not a new human rights determination or publication authorization.

The candidate remains nonaccepted. Human textual acceptance, canonical data or IR mutation, and publication are separate and absent.
