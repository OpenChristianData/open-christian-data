# LIB-071 retained Gutenberg and Internet Archive witnesses

This source packet binds ten complete retained UTF-8 text files: four Project
Gutenberg transcriptions and six Internet Archive OCR witnesses. The candidate
projects every physical source line, including title matter, credits,
advertisements, OCR corruption, Project Gutenberg wrappers and license text,
and Internet Archive digitization notices. No legacy parser section boundary
or generated JSON reading is treated as source authority.

The four Project Gutenberg texts are eBooks 11449, 16739, 23334, and 57121.
Project Gutenberg asks redistributors to preserve its license and identify the
source. The complete retained wrapper is preserved inside each corresponding
TEI work, and the current license information is available at
[gutenberg.org/policy/license.html](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html).
Project Gutenberg is not affiliated with Open Christian Data.

The six Internet Archive OCR witnesses retain their own scan and digitization
credits in the projected source lines. Their collection identifiers and source
URLs are recorded in the reconstruction profile and TEI header. Internet
Archive is not asserted to be the print publisher or textual editor.

Three edition descriptions remain deliberately unresolved. Pink's retained
text is a revised witness: it contains a “Foreword to the Second Edition” and
says the revision followed the first edition by two years, despite a retained
1918 copyright line. Smith's retained title matter says “New, Revised and
Enlarged Edition”; the OCR copyright line appears to say 1888 and does not
support the former c.1883 description. Murray's retained *With Christ in the
School of Prayer* title matter identifies Fleming H. Revell but does not verify
an 1885 scan edition. These source-bound descriptions do not alter canonical
data.

All ten outputs remain `accepted_ir:not_accepted`, require human textual and
edition review, and are `publication:not_ready`.
