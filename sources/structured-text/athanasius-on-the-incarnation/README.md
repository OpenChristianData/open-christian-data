xxv of `raw/ccel/npnf2/npnf204.xml`. The retained title page, editorial front
matter, prolegomena, and index divisions i through v and xxvi remain outside the

with reading the translations throughout. Work-level credit remains exact and
non-inferred: H. Ellershaw translated the *Life of Antony*; Newman's *de
Synodis* translation was more closely revised; the Antioch/Africa group was
independently translated and collated with Bright without naming its translator;
and Miss Payne Smith revised the Oxford translation of the bulk of the Festal
Letters.
