# A Serious Call to a Devout and Holy Life source note

The retained authority is the single CCEL ThML witness at
`raw/ccel/law/serious_call.xml`, sourced from
<https://www.ccel.org/ccel/law/serious_call.xml>. Its source-specific Dublin
Core subject list includes `Proofed`. CCEL's [cataloging guide](https://www.ccel.org/help/catalog.html)
defines `Proofed` as text known to have been proofread and clean. This work is
therefore admitted as a proofed text witness; it is not treated as uncorrected
OCR. No new proofreading against page images was performed here.

The retained ThML file contains one title page, 24 chapters, Appendices A–C,
Indexes, and Acknowledgements in 30 ordered divisions. The title page and
Appendix A support a 1729 claim; Appendix B records a conflicting 1728 claim.
The exact print-edition relationship is unresolved and no print collation was
performed.

CCEL asks for acknowledgment as “sourced via CCEL.org” and says not to sell
CCEL files or derivatives of its formatting. A shared data-side attribution
notice is maintained at `data/structured-text/README.md`. Rights clearance,
human acceptance, canonical promotion, and publication authorization remain
unresolved.
