CCEL NPNF2 XML members `npnf206.xml`, `npnf209.xml`, `npnf212.xml`, and
`npnf213.xml`. The retained XML is the textual and metadata authority. Existing
JSON records and parsers identify routes but do not supply reconstruction text.

The retained electronic headers identify Philip Schaff as series editor and
state `Public Domain`. The retained Jerome title-page and prefatory material

are preserved exactly in candidate provenance and source-description notes;
they are source-level evidence and are not converted into unsupported
work-specific translator assignments.

the document shell, title pages, prefaces, container divisions, siblings, and
indexes that lie outside those exact roots. Technical verification does not
constitute human textual acceptance or publication authorization. Publication
remains `not_ready`.
