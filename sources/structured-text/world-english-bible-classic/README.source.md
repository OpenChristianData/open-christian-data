# World English Bible Classic — source-bound TEI candidate

This is a nonaccepted technical reconstruction of the retained official eBible `eng-web` edition, identified by the provider receipt as the 2020 stable text, American English, classic Yahweh form. Its text authority is the retained USFM archive; the retained USFX archive is an independently traversed comparison encoding of the same edition. Both ZIP envelopes and extracted members are accounted for. The candidate preserves the USFM book-member order, including FRT front matter and GLO glossary; the USFX encoding places GLO at the end, and the crosswalk uses source book IDs rather than assuming positional identity.

The candidate preserves source wording and visible structures without normalization or editorial correction. The USFM source lines remain individually source-pointed and byte-text exact in `source-usfm-raw` notes. Psalm 119 acrostic headings and Song of Songs speaker labels remain separate structural carriers in their source order. Notes, cross-references, Strong-linked words, paragraph and poetry markers, and front/back matter remain within their source-defined boundaries.

## Provider title-use restriction

The retained eBible copyright member identifies the text as public domain and identifies “World English Bible” as a trademark. It states: “All we ask is that if you CHANGE the actual text of the World English Bible in any way, you not call the result the World English Bible any more.” The source wording is unchanged here; the provider title is preserved without invented title edits. This notice is reproduced to keep the provider restriction visible to reviewers. It does not assert broader rights clearance or authorize publication.

This package is a source-bound candidate only. Human acceptance, rights review, catalog integration, and publication remain open.
