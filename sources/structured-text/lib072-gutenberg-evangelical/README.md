# LIB-072 retained Gutenberg and Internet Archive witnesses

This source packet binds six complete retained UTF-8 files to five works: one
Project Gutenberg transcription and five Internet Archive OCR members. Every
physical line is projected, including title matter, scan credits,
advertisements, OCR corruption, the Project Gutenberg wrapper and license, and
Internet Archive digitization notices. Current JSON and the legacy parser are
diagnostics only, not textual authority.

Project Gutenberg eBook 25709 asks redistributors to preserve its license and
identify the source. The complete retained wrapper is included in the TEI; the
current license is at
[gutenberg.org/policy/license.html](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html).
Project Gutenberg is not affiliated with Open Christian Data.

The Internet Archive witnesses retain their own collection and digitization
credits. Internet Archive is not asserted to be the print publisher or textual
editor. “Lectures to My Students” intentionally consists of two independently
pinned members in declared Series I then Series II order; its frozen map hash
is the legacy deterministic aggregate of those two ordered member hashes.

The All of Grace OCR title page appears to read 1836, which conflicts with the
work's known chronology and is not silently corrected. The exact edition of
that scan remains unresolved. The other witness descriptions state only dates
and publishers directly present in retained title/copyright matter.

All five outputs remain `accepted_ir:not_accepted`, require human textual,
edition, and rights review, and are `publication:not_ready`.
