# Luther *Bondage of the Will* retained-source evidence

This file accompanies a nonaccepted reconstruction candidate for *The Bondage of the Will* by Martin Luther. The exact retained witness is a UTF-8 text extraction of thirteen ordered pages supplied by Reformed Presbyterian Church (Covenanted), commonly identified as Covenanter.org. It identifies Henry Cole as translator and describes the English translation as published in London in 1823.

Provider index: https://www.covenanter.org/reformed/2015/7/8/martin-luthers-book-concerning-the-bondage-of-the-will

The retained witness preserves the exact URL and title of each of its thirteen component pages. The candidate provenance and source-accountability files retain all thirteen URLs, every physical line, and every CRLF line terminator in source order.

The retained text contains no copyright notice, transcription license, permission statement, required-credit wording, or link-back requirement. The age of Luther's work and Cole's translation does not by itself establish rights in the Covenanter transcription. Human rights review, textual acceptance, and publication authorization remain unresolved. Status: `publication:not_ready`.
