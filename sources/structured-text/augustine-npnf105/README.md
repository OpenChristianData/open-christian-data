# Augustine NPNF105 retained-source reconstruction

This source-family packet binds the twelve LIB-004 works to the complete retained
`div1` scopes x through xxi of `raw/ccel/npnf1/npnf105.xml`. The single retained
member has SHA-256
`224f0e6a188ddfe691c1433c8db9a883755a9f030b71fede644d4af6c18ee63f`.

The retained header identifies the underlying text as public domain and the
print source as *New York: Christian Literature Publishing Co., 1886*. The
retained electronic edition is sourced via [CCEL.org](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/npnf105.html).
CCEL permits parsing its ThML; do not sell CCEL files or derivatives of CCEL's
formatting. The reconstruction preserves text and meaningful source structure;
it does not claim CCEL formatting as an original work.

Philip Schaff is retained as editor. The retained title page credits Peter Holmes
and Robert Ernest Wallis as translators, and Benjamin B. Warfield as translation
reviser and introduction author. Retained `div1` ix supplies work-specific
allocations for four works: *Grace and Free-Will* to Holmes; and *Rebuke and
Grace*, *The Predestination of the Saints* with its *Gift of Perseverance* second
book, and *Against Two Letters of the Pelagians* to Wallis. The retained contents note at `iii-p74` additionally assigns the asterisk-marked
works to Wallis and the other listed treatises to Holmes. The unstarred entries
at `iii-p10`, `iii-p15`, `iii-p19`, `iii-p24`, `iii-p28`, `iii-p33`, `iii-p38`
and `iii-p46` therefore establish Holmes for the other eight assigned works.
