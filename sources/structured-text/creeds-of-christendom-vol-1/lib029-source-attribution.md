# LIB-029 source attribution evidence

The retained electronic witness is the CCEL ThML edition of *The Creeds of
Christendom, Vol. I: The History of Creeds* by Philip Schaff. The source URL is
[CCEL.org](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds1.xml). The candidate retains
the source hash and the explicit eight-chapter source scope; it does not assert
that this electronic witness has been collated against a print edition. Rights,
edition identity, human acceptance, and publication remain separate review
decisions.
