# Chrysostom NPNF retained-source reconstruction

The LIB-043 packet binds seven John Chrysostom works to four complete retained
CCEL ThML authorities: `npnf110.xml`, `npnf111.xml`, `npnf112.xml`, and
`npnf114.xml`. The exact assigned roots are NPNF110 `div1` iii (Matthew),
NPNF111 vi–vii (Acts and Romans), NPNF112 iv–v (First and Second Corinthians),
and NPNF114 iv–v (John and Hebrews). Front matter and indexes outside those
roots are explicitly accounted rather than silently treated as work content.

The retained work roots include source apparatus omitted by the legacy parser:
Matthew's title page, Oxford preface, and introductory essay; Romans' preface
and argument; First Corinthians' argument; John's title page and preface; and
Hebrews' title page, preface, introduction, and argument.

Retained credits govern the candidate. They identify George Prevost and M. B.
Riddle for Matthew; J. Walker, J. Sheppard, H. Browne, and George B. Stevens
for Acts; J. B. Morris, W. H. Simcox, and Stevens for Romans; Talbot W.
Chambers for both Corinthians works; G. T. Stupart, J. G. Hickley, and Philip
Schaff for John; and T. Keble, his revising brother (the Vicar of Hursley), and
Frederic Gardiner for Hebrews. Conflicting legacy scalar metadata is diagnostic
only and is not copied into the candidate.

The electronic witnesses are sourced via [CCEL.org](https://www.ccel.org/).
Their headers state Public Domain. CCEL permits parsing its ThML; do not sell
CCEL files or derivatives of CCEL formatting. This reconstruction preserves
text and meaningful source structure and does not claim CCEL formatting as an
original work.

All seven works remain `accepted_ir:not_accepted`, `human_review:required`, and
`publication:not_ready`. Human textual acceptance, a final rights decision,
and publication authorization are separate and absent.
