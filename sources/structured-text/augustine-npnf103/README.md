# Augustine NPNF103 retained-source reconstruction

The LIB-039 candidate binds exactly the sixteen direct `div2` work roots
`iv.i` through `iv.vii` and `v.i` through `v.ix` of retained
`raw/ccel/npnf1/npnf103.xml`. The title page, edition preface, contents,
doctrinal and moral wrapper divisions, and five retained index divisions remain
outside the exact work projection and are explicitly accounted in the packet.

The retained title page identifies Philip Schaff as editor. Work-level credits
are taken from the retained contents and work headings: Arthur West Haddan
translated *On the Holy Trinity*, whose translation William G. T. Shedd revised
and annotated while supplying an introductory essay; J. F. Shaw translated
*The Enchiridion*; S. D. F. Salmond translated *On the Catechising of the
Uninstructed* and *A Treatise on Faith and the Creed*; and retained headings
credit C. L. Cornish and H. Browne across the remaining assigned works. Two
source conflicts remain explicit: *On the Creed* has C. L. Cornish in its work
heading but H. Brown in the contents, while *Of Holy Virginity* has C. I.
Cornish in its work heading but C. L. Cornish in the contents. No identity or
typographical correction is inferred.

The retained electronic edition is sourced via
[CCEL.org](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/npnf103.html). Its header states
Public Domain. CCEL permits parsing its ThML; do not sell CCEL files or
derivatives of CCEL formatting. The candidate preserves textual and structural
content and does not claim CCEL formatting as an original work.
