# The Life of the Rev. George Whitefield, B.A. — retained source

This is one complete biography by L. Tyerman in two ordered volumes, published by Hodder and Stoughton in 1876 and 1877. The primary title pages identify a two-volume work. The retained electronic witnesses are Project Gutenberg eBooks 44280 and 62023, with their source ZIPs, HTML, and image files. This is Tyerman's biography, not Whitefield's collected Works.

Volume I credits Heather Clark, Julia Neufeld, and the Online Distributed Proofreading Team. Volume II credits Richard Hulse, Brian Wilson, Heather Clark, and that team. Both say their source images were made available by The Internet Archive.
