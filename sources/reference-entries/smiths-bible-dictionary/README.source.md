# Smith's Bible Dictionary — retained historical definitions and source credit

The accepted electronic witness contains all 4,560 ordered entries and 5,362
definition blocks of the retained [JWBickel/BibleDictionaries](https://huggingface.co/datasets/JWBickel/BibleDictionaries)
Smith member. JWBickel supplied the structured JSONL. Its terms, definitions,
numbered link markers, and source order remain unchanged in the accepted master
and projected records. No missing entry is silently supplied from another text.

Every retained headword and definition was compared in source order with
[CCEL's Smith's Bible Dictionary electronic text](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/smith_w/bibledict.html).
CCEL's title page and electronic print-source field identify William Smith and
1884; its header calls the underlying text Public Domain. All 4,560 retained
headwords match literally. Definition word sequences match after removing only
generated numeric link labels for the comparison; punctuation, spacing, and
electronic glyph differences were not used to rewrite the retained wording.
The CCEL comparison text has one additional final entry, “Zuzim, The.” The
selected scope is the complete retained electronic witness, not a claim to a
complete or independently collated 1884 print edition.

The matched historical definition words support a United States public-domain
text assessment. JWBickel's retained provider card supplies no source-file
license; none is invented here. The comparison establishes historical text
lineage, not a transfer of CCEL's website or file permissions to JWBickel.
No modern authored definition was found in the complete word comparison.
The selected rows are plain terms and definitions; CCEL presentation and
website material are not selected. Exact print identity, global rights, and
public deployment remain separate decisions.

The earlier October 8 hold for absent provider text terms is superseded only
for this source-bound historical-word selection. Keep the downloader-visible
credit and scope notice at `data/reference_entries/smiths-bible-dictionary/README.md`.
