# York tractate IV source and attribution

The retained openMGH electronic witness provides an explicit fourth titled tractate, De consecratione pontificum et regum. The reconstruction preserves its entire native part, including the unfinished ending and six literal dots. It does not restore or certify the complete original composition.

The electronic publication statement and print bibliography remain in the faithful TEI. Its publishers request attribution for distributed copies and derivatives. The human-visible distribution credit is [the data notice](../../../data/prose/york-tractate-consecration-of-bishops-and-kings/README.md).

The source statement assigns CC BY 4.0 to annotations added by Monumenta Germaniae Historica and the Bavarian State Library, Munich, under digital MGH, funded by the German Research Foundation. It states that no rights are claimed on the text itself, believed to be public domain. This records the statement; human rights clearance and publication authorization remain separate.

The selected human-visible data notice is `data/prose/york-tractate-consecration-of-bishops-and-kings/README.md`.
