# The Catholic Encyclopedia — retained New Advent article text

The accepted source is the partial New Advent electronic cohort acquired in
September 2026. The historical work identifier ends in `vol01`, but this accepted
cohort contains article-local citations to multiple printed volumes, including
the 1914 Index and 1922 Supplement. It is not a Volume I-only or complete
printed-set witness. New Advent is credited as the electronic transcription
provider, with contributors identified on their own article pages. Its website
asserts a 2026 copyright for site material.

The proposed private offline release selects only 11,629 source pages with one
article-local citation dated 1907–1922. It retains historical article wording,
headings, source bibliography, and each page's factual author, volume, date,
transcription, dedication, and ecclesiastical approval credits where present.
It omits 48 navigation or index pages, three numeric source-history or address
pages without a unique article citation, and the unavailable-source register.
Within selected articles it omits modern donation/contact/footer prose, site
formatting and link targets, and image pixels and alternative descriptions.
These remain accounted for in the exact private accepted reconstruction and
public transform inventory. Source gaps, including failed page requests and
unavailable images, remain explicit qualifications.

The article-local historical dates support a United States public-domain text
disposition under the U.S. Copyright Office's published-before-1931 guidance.
This does not claim rights in New Advent's website, presentation, images, or
independently authored modern prose, and it does not assert exact print
collation or worldwide rights. The retained source's contributor credits are
preserved visibly in each selected article. Public uploading needs its own
authorization.

The selected public notice must be delivered at
`data/reference_entries/catholic-encyclopedia-vol01/README.md` and bound by
its exact file digest in the release selection.
