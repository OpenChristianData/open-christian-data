# City of God Reconstruction print evidence

This source record binds the retained public-domain scans used to reconstruct and
verify two separate editions of the Dods/Wilson/Smith translation:

- T. & T. Clark, Edinburgh, 1871, issued as two physical volumes; and
- NPNF First Series volume II, Christian Literature Company, Buffalo, 1887.

The scan files, Internet Archive metadata, page maps, plain OCR, and
coordinate-bearing OCR are retained under
`raw/internet-archive/city-of-god/`. The raw directory is intentionally ignored;
`print-acquisitions.json` is the durable identity, URL, byte-count, and SHA-256
record.

CCEL's separate “New York ... 1890” print-basis statement is preserved as a
contradicted unresolved source claim. Current primary evidence does not establish a
physical 1890 City of God issue, so no 1890 edition or master is created.

Primary-source research and duplicate-scan controls are documented in
`research/2026-07-23-city-of-god-print-evidence-acquisition.md`.
