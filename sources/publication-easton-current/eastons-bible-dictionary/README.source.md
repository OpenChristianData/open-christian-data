# Easton's Bible Dictionary — retained historical definitions and credit

This selection contains all 3,963 entries and 7,464 definition blocks in the
retained JWBickel/BibleDictionaries JSONL witness. JWBickel is credited for supplying
that structured electronic witness. The accepted reconstruction and selected
records preserve its wording, ordering, repeated Kadesh and Salmon entries,
cross-reference numbers and definition boundaries. No dictionary illustration is
included. The text is not represented as a complete independently collated print edition.

A full comparison of every retained definition to [CCEL's third-edition 1897
electronic text](https://ccel.org/e/easton/ebd2/cache/ebd2.html3) finds the same
wording after comparison-only whitespace and numbered cross-reference normalization.
CCEL identifies M. G. Easton's *Illustrated Bible Dictionary*, third edition,
Thomas Nelson, 1897, and labels the electronic text public domain. Its earlier
[electronic edition](https://www.ccel.org/e/easton/ebd/ebd3.html) expressly says
“Public Domain -- Copy Freely.” The retained provider set omits “Zuzims,” which
is present in the comparison edition. That difference is disclosed, not silently
filled from a different source. Source-faithful reconstruction of the retained
JSONL and completeness of the 1897 print dictionary are different claims.

The selected definitions are treated as historical public-domain text in the
United States. This does not invent a license from JWBickel: its retained dataset
card states none. All retained wording was compared to the identified historical
electronic text; JSON serialization, paragraph grouping and numbered references
do not by themselves establish new authored prose. No modern authored definition
was found in that complete comparison. The primary comparison is evidence, not
a replacement source or a transfer of CCEL's website license to JWBickel.
